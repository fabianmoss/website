{{< research_intro name="computational-musicology" >}}
Computational musicology applies computational methods—from corpus statistics to machine learning and Artificial Intelligence and Music—to analyze musical corpora. This interdisciplinary field bridges computer science and musicology, enabling researchers to discover patterns, trace stylistic evolution, and quantify musical features at scales beyond human perception. This includes research at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Music, where machine learning helps to model musical structure and stylistic change.
