abstract: >-
  This dataset provides manual annotations and duration-weighted pitch-class profiles for 219 Chinese folk melodies from the Essen Folksong Collection. Each melody is annotated with its diao category, gong and diao tou (final-tone category). The collection comprises 156 xia zhi, 52 zheng sheng and 11 qing shang melodies. The dataset accompanies the study: Meng, S, Moss, F, C., and Rohrmeier M. A. (2026) Revisiting Tong Yun San Gong Theory: Corpus Evidence for the Distinctions among Three Chinese Heptatonic Diao.
